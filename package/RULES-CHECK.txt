0.80.0 changes no rules of the game: the Tournament (league mode, see the
README). These are my choices for the league's own rules - please check them:

L1  "Win before round 7": the winner won in round 1 to 6.
L2  "Knock a player out": goes to the player whose turn it was when the other
    player was eliminated.
L3  "Keep every promise you make": you made at least one promise in the game
    and broke none (an X-1 payback you didn't send counts as broken).
L4  "Support for the Throne from 3 players", "Occupy Mecatol Rex and Styx",
    "Occupy all planets in the Fracture": counted at the end of the game.
L5  Places: the winner is always 1st; the rest by victory points; players knocked
    out share the last places. Shared places split their points, rounded up
    (two players sharing 2nd: (4 + 3) / 2 = 3.5 -> 4 each).
L6  The league's end: as soon as anyone reaches the target, the most points
    wins (you, when you are level with a rival). After the last game the most
    points wins the same way. A lost league starts again at game 1 with 0
    points; your rivals keep their memory of you.
L7  "Rising" bots (Grand league, Ashfall Crucible): Normal for the first third
    of the league's games, Hard for the second, Competitive for the last.
L8  A rival's grudge: +5 B-Factor toward you for every promise you ever broke
    to it, +1 for every game you finished above it, at most +25.
L9  Leagues without a faction draft: you pick once from players + 3 factions
    (the Crystal Gauntlet: from every faction it deals) and keep it; each rival
    keeps its own faction when the league deals it, the other bots get one at
    random from the league's pool.
L10 Your rival for a game: the rival nearest you in points (on a tie, the one
    above you); it always gets a seat at your table, the other seats go to the
    rivals who have played fewest games in this league.

YOUR 0.44.8 ANSWERS - WHAT I DID
 W1, W6, W7, W9-W13. As you said - no change.
 W2.  The Living Rules FAQ version: The Alastor (Nekro flagship) and the Z-Grav
      flip are chosen BEFORE Assault Cannon, so ground forces fighting through
      The Alastor and Z-Grav / Eidolon Maximum mechs count toward the 3 ships,
      and may be picked as the ship Assault Cannon destroys.
 W3.  The start of a space combat now follows LRR 1.19: the active player
      resolves their Assault Cannon / Dimensional Splicer first, then the other
      player. A Creuss with both is asked which goes first.
 W4.  Proxima Targeting VI "resolves BOMBARDMENT", so it is treated as a
      bombardment for what stops one: it can't be used against a planet with the
      opponent's Planetary Shield (unless your war sun is there, or the shield is
      down) or against a cultural planet under Conventions of War. The Bastion's
      mechs may sustain against the backlash (as now).
 W5.  You choose the planet that gets the extra bombardment die (Plasma Scoring
      and the like) when more than one is bombarded.
 W8.  Duranium Armor asks which unit to repair when more than one kind is
      damaged.
 W14. Yes, both changed: (1) a unit upgrade one prerequisite short asks whether
      to use AI Development Algorithm before a Discordant Stars waiver that
      would be used up (bots always do); (2) a space combat where nobody can
      win (20 rounds in a row without a single unit lost - Duranium Armor with
      Non-Euclidean Shielding) ends as the FAQ says: the attacker retreats if
      it can, or is destroyed.
 P1.  Games without the Codex or Thunder's Edge play the original Wormhole
      Generator: at the start of the status phase you may place or move a
      Creuss wormhole token (no action, no exhausting). The card text follows.
 P2.  A Creuss token may go into an inactive Wormhole Nexus (not the Creuss
      home); when the nexus becomes active the token goes back to the Creuss.
 P3, P5, P7. As you said.
 P4.  The difference: "End turn" stops after your first action - you are still
      in the round and get more turns later. "Pass" means you are done for the
      round (no more turns; you can't pass before using your strategy card).
      Living Rules 3.1 names three kinds of action - strategic, tactical and
      component - and passing isn't one of them (3.2: you pass when you can't
      or won't act), so an extra action from Fleet Logistics, Jupiter Brain,
      Master Plan or Minister of War offers "End turn" but not "Pass". Not
      changed - see Q1 below.
 P6.  Movement boosts now stack on one ship unless a card says otherwise:
      Gravity Drive + Ionian Fuel Refinery + Lightning Drives (and the like)
      can give one ship +2 or more. The move screen shows "+2 move" etc.
 P8.  A carrier that needs the +1 for a detour to pick up units now uses up a
      boost. Shrouded Skirmishers: my judgement is to keep it as it is - only
      ships of the active player can have arrived in the active system during
      the action, so it works for the active player.
 G1.  Radical Advancement may swap faction technologies too, and may replace
      itself (e.g. for a biotic technology with two prerequisites). A faction
      technology taken has to be one the player could research (their own).
 G2-G5. As you said. G6: done in 0.44.7 (Pilgrimage Beacons triggers on every
      specialty planet you exhaust on your turn).
 C1-C3, C6, C8, C10. As you said.
 C4.  Parallel Production may use the system that just produced as one of its
      two systems.
 C5.  The Roh'Dhna may pick themselves for Contractual Obligations. When a
      person is picked by someone else, they see a notice they confirm before
      producing.
 C7.  The feed (and the choice) now say that the space dock produces by the
      planet's new resource value.
 C9.  Transit Diodes moves the Titans' Hel-Titans too.
 C11. Inheritance Systems is now offered whenever you research a technology
      with prerequisites still to cover (you're asked whether to use it), and
      with Reveal Prototype (4 + 2 resources). Recursive Worm can now pick a
      player with no tokens in reinforcements - they give one from a pool
      (LRR 20.3b), as with Local Contracts.
 B1.  Professional Intrigue and Insurrectionist Networking may send their
      infantry onto an enemy planet with nobody on it - the planet is simply
      taken.
 B2.  Stabilization Arrays: each Eye of the Curator fighter takes half a slot
      of capacity (carried, or kept in a space area); in the fleet pool they
      count as normal fighters.
 B3.  Bannerhalls give 1 vote each, like planet cards, and ready with the
      planets at the end of the agenda phase.
 B4.  Checked: nothing else gives the Dih-Mohn a production in the middle of a
      space combat, and the choice is offered to the player each time the
      trigger happens. The "produce beside other players' ships" part also
      works as a standing rule for their normal productions. No change.
 B5.  No change needed: the game treats both flagships as the same piece, so
      after one is destroyed the Celdauri may build a flagship again - which
      stands for the Celagrom too.
 B6-B8. As you said.
 B9.  All added: the Titans with Slumberstate Computing may ask another player
      (from the action menu, without using the action) to let them place a
      sleeper token on one of that player's planets - the owner confirms;
      the Yssaril with Deepgloom Executable may let another player use
      Scheming when they draw action cards (the drawer asks, the Yssaril
      confirm, then a trade outside the usual limit); each Omni-Forgeworld is

      Valefar Assimilator Z now gets Hil Colish (the delta wormhole), The

 R1.  As you play it - no change.
