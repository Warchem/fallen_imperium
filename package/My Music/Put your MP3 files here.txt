MY MUSIC - Fallen Imperium's music player
==========================================

Put your own MP3 files in this folder. You can also make folders inside it
(one per album, say) - the game looks two folders deep.

Then start the game and open:  Options -> Music player

There you choose what plays during a game:
  - Game soundtrack (the game's own music - the default)
  - My Music (your songs)
  - Both

and whether they play in order or shuffled. Tick a song off to skip it, or
listen to one right there. Press Y (or click "Look for new songs") after
adding files while the game is running.

The title screen keeps its own theme.

Song names come from the files' tags (title and artist). Files without tags
show their file name - "Artist - Title.mp3" is read as artist and title.

Only MP3 files are played. When the game is updated, this folder and your
songs stay where they are.
